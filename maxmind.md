# MaxMind

MaxMind is an American technology company that develops and maintains IP intelligence and online fraud detection services. It is best known for its GeoIP and GeoLite databases, which map IP addresses to geographic locations, and its minFraud service for e-commerce risk scoring.

Key facts
Founded: 2002

Founders: Thomas Mather, Peter Zotov

Headquarters: Waltham, Massachusetts, U.S.

Products: GeoIP2, GeoLite2, minFraud

Industry: IP geolocation, fraud prevention

Geolocation Data Services
MaxMind’s GeoIP product line provides IP-to-location mapping used in content localization, network analytics, and cybersecurity. GeoIP2 and the free GeoLite2 databases link IP addresses to data such as country, city, and ASN (Autonomous System Number). These databases are updated weekly to reflect IP address reassignments and network changes.

Fraud Detection Solutions
The company’s minFraud service helps online businesses assess transaction risk by analyzing IP reputation, email address, device, and proxy usage. It generates a risk score that merchants can use to flag or block suspicious purchases. This service is widely adopted in e-commerce, online gaming, and digital advertising industries to reduce chargebacks and payment fraud.

Privacy and Data Governance
MaxMind emphasizes compliance with privacy regulations like the GDPR and CCPA. Its databases use anonymized and aggregated data, and users must agree to licensing terms that limit personal data exposure. In 2020, the company addressed public concerns about data accuracy and retention, reinforcing its privacy policies and opt-out mechanisms.

Market Role
MaxMind is a major supplier of IP geolocation data, used in both commercial and open-source applications. Its free GeoLite databases are integrated into numerous web analytics tools and network applications, making it one of the most widely relied-upon sources for IP-based location intelligence.
